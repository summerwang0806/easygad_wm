D.3 Paraphrasing
We paraphrase only the reasoning body between<think>and</think>, preserving the tags and
the final answer, then reapply the standard quality filter before distillation. A same-family (Qwen)
paraphraser rewrites the traces under mild, moderate and aggressive instructions atT=0.5,0.8and
1.0respectively, with top-p=0.95and at most8,192generated tokens. Each setting trains20keyed
students. Rewriting costs the adversary no capability: mean GSM8K accuracy exceeds77%at every
strength and window against68to69%for the unmodified control, and every strength stays above its
matched clean student in the paired per-key comparison.
