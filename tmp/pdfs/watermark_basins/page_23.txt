B.6 Calibration of the Rankp-Value
By the exchangeability argument of App. A, thresholding atp≤αshould falsely accuse anαfraction
of models that were not distilled from the protected teacher, which is what §3.1 relies on when it
reads operating points off the nominalp-value. We test it on100deployed keys and65clean model-
temperature cells held out from the64used to fit the task subspace (App. B.5), ranking each key
among the full1,000-key bank. Figure 7 plots the empirical rate againstαfor each deployed key, for
WM-Basin (Fig. 7a) and for the black-box baseline (Fig. 7b).
The per-key false-positive distribution.Table 11 reports the empirical false-positive rate atα=5%over

subspace projection and under the same4-fold cross-fitting by base model as App. B.5. The typical
key is calibrated and the tail is not, which is the distinction the mean over the bank hides: the median
