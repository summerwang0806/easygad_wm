How many components to remove.We chooseqon clean models alone, and we read the false-positive
rate of each single deployed key. Figure 6 sweepsqfromno PCAtoq=100and reads three keys of
the1,000-key bank against TPR: the typical key, the key at the95th percentile of the per-key rate
in terms of FPR, and the worst key which has the highest FPR. Without the projection those three
disagree completely, the typical key firing on no clean cell at all while the95th-percentile key fires on
38to44%of them. Removingq=100directions closes that spread onto the nominal line and lifts TPR
atα=5%from70.2to91.7atn-gram=1. A smallqdoes neither, because the leading directions carry
watermark signal as well as shared task structure: the projection pays for itself only fromq≈10, after
which power climbs monotonically. We deployq=100, and App. B.6 reports the per-key calibration
that remains at that setting.
