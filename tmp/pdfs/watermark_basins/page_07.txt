Baseline.We compare with the black-box sampled-token detector of Sander et al. (2024), to our
knowledge the only existing detector for this setting. It reads the same suspect text as WM-Basin, so
every comparison is paired. We run it under its own protocol with both filters that give it power: a
position is scored only when its context window occurs in a held-out corpus of5,000teacher traces
regenerated under the key being scored, and each distinct (context, token) pair is scored once. Its
p-value is computed analytically from the score’s null distribution rather than by ranking decoys, and
we threshold both detectors at the samep-value. App. B.4 gives the test used for each watermark
scheme and the remaining settings.
