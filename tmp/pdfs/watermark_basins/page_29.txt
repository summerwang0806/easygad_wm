D.1 Compression
This section gives the protocol behind Tab. 6 and the weight-space displacement of every operation.
We apply per-output-channel symmetric int8, uniform group-64 int4, nf4, or10to30%unstructured
magnitude pruning to100keyed students at eachn-gram window, regenerating detector inputs after
each operation. Quantized weights are dequantized to fp16 for evaluation, and pruning excludeslm_-
headandembed_tokens. A model counts as usable when its mean GSM8K accuracy is at least0.9×
the distilled baseline, which int8 and prune10 clear and int4, nf4, prune20 and prune30 do not. The
nonerow of Tab. 6 is the uncompressed reference rather than a compression condition, so its black-
box cells were never re-read under the token matching the six operation rows use; that row therefore
supports no black-box comparison, either with its own WM-Basin cells or down the column.
Compression costs the watermark only where it costs the model. Int8 and prune10 retain both utility
and strong WM-Basin detection, and the larger detection losses occur only beyond the usability gate,
with nf4 and prune20 the two operations where the gradient signal outlasts utility (Fig. 10 atn-gram=3;
Tabs. 6 and 16 at all three windows). The weight-space displacement is ordered the same way: the
along component of every compression operation stays below0.11against orthogonal movement of14
to152, and grows with the pruning fraction. Therefore, compression does not move the model outside
the watermark basin, and detection remains effective.
D.2 Continued Fine-Tuning and RL
This section gives the training recipes behind Tab. 7 and the weight-space displacement of every arm.
We continue training the keyed Qwen2.5-3B students of eachn-gram window on clean data. We then
regenerate suspect text and re-measure detection, utility and geometry. Continued SFT uses one of
two clean corpora. Thereasoncorpus is1,900traces,950code and950science, from open-r1/Mixture-
of-Thoughts (Hugging Face, 2025). Themultilingualcorpus is1,206traces on math questions in eight
languages (Chen et al., 2024). Both pass the think-closure and repetition filters of App. B.2.
SFT keeps the distillation adapter shape. It runs2epochs at global batch16and maximum sequence
length8192, on a cosine learning rate of2×10−5 with0.05warmup. Continued RL uses GRPO (Shao
et al., 2024). The in-domain arm optimises gsm8k with a verifiable correctness reward. The two
cross-domain arms use learning rate5×10−6 on rule-verified K&K logic puzzles (Xie et al., 2025) or
on RLVR-IFeval instruction following (Lambert et al., 2024). Both cross-domain rewards are all-or-
nothing: K&K requires every inhabitant to be labeled correctly in the final boxed answer, and IFEval
