C.3 Watermark Scheme
Table 4 replaces the teacher’s TextSeal decoder with three other watermarking schemes. TextSeal, the
dual-key scheme used everywhere else in the paper (Sander et al., 2026), draws each token’s Gumbel
noise from one of two keys with probabilityα=0.1; GumbelMax (Aaronson, 2022) is the single-key
Gumbel scheme; and SynthID (Dathathri et al., 2024) resolves each position through a tournament
of depth10between keyed g-values. KGW (Kirchenbauer et al., 2023) instead adds a fixed logit bias
δ=2.0to a keyed half of the vocabulary (γ=0.5) at every position, the one scheme here that modifies
the teacher’s logits rather than the sampling rule. Each is generated atn-gram=1,2and3and distilled
into50students, with all detectors reading the same≈1M-token budget, the reference bank rebuilt
per scheme and window, and everything else at the defaults of Tab. 8.
